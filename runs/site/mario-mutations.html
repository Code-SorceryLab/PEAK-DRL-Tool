Does a playtest notice one small change?

Every level of each game gets five one-change copies: placed by hand on the evaluation level, by rule on the others. The original's agents, frozen, replay each copy and are compared with their own play on the original: a sealed or harder level should be caught, an off-route tile should stay quiet, and the cosmetic copy must play out identically. Agents retrained on a copy answer one question only: can it still be finished? (Retrained agents are too noisy to detect with: on the unchanged level, new seeds set off the rule 22 times in 35.)

Why retraining is not the detector

The original retrained with three new seeds and played like a mutant, over 35 comparisons (a level, or a Mega Man section, whose original is solved). Every signal it raises is a false alarm: seed noise. Replay has none, because frozen agents replay attempt for attempt.
